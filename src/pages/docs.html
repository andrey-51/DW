Functional overview

Data Wave CDP is designed to collect data, aggregate information about users and communicate with them.

Data collection

The CDP extracts customer data from multiple sources, such as transaction systems, online behavior, the call center, demographic data or POS.

Identity management

The CDP identifies customers across multiple channels through deterministic matching and profile stitching based on identifiers in the incoming data. It creates and maintains a persistent customer identifier.

Segmentation and enrichment

Customers can be segmented, including in real time. Profiles can be enriched with insights from third-party DMP providers.

Website and recommendations

  • Website behavior management
  • Banner management
  • Recommendation systems
  • No-code personalization of banners, sections and individual blocks for user segments
