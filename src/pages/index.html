What is Datawave CDP?

A customer-centric platform that combines advanced data analytics with multichannel campaign execution.

The platform aggregates and analyzes all types of customer data from multiple sources and uses it to build a single customer profile.

Maximize profit and optimize marketing spend by focusing on the right customers with the right content at the right time with DataWave.
