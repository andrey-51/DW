About Datawave
Datawave is a high-tech tool for boosting sales and service efficiency. The platform aggregates and analyzes various types of customer data from multiple sources and uses it to build a single customer profile.
What you can do with a single customer profile:

Precise segmentation

In-depth analytics

Launching personalized marketing campaigns

Measuring marketing campaign effectiveness

A useful tool for businesses with multiple sales channels and an extensive communications network. It serves as a single control center and a hub for collecting statistics and monitoring customer service quality.

Additional benefits of the Datawave platform
PRODUCT CUSTOMIZATION
The DataWave platform is built on a microservice architecture, which makes product customization fast and efficient. You choose the functionality you need, which we can then modify or extend.
Adapting the product to your needs
Each project is assigned a dedicated team that fully adapts the product to your needs. This way, you get only what you need and can configure the product without tying up additional resources.
Behavior management on the website and in the mobile app
The omnichannel platform lets you manage customer interactions through a range of engagement mechanics while keeping communication seamless. DataWave lets you predict user behavior and manage it online. No programming knowledge is required: everything is managed in a no-code format.
ML training and analytics system
ML attribution has many practical applications in a wide range of business areas that need to identify the most effective marketing channels for reaching their goals. DataWave ML offers cost-per-click optimization, goal-completion optimization and ROI, lookalike, CPL and CPA optimization.
Recommendations
DataWave lets you create product recommendations on your website, in your app or on POS terminals. The recommendation engine can run when specific actions are taken or goals are met; it can also be driven by ML models or by the behavior of similar customer segments.
Automation for business goals
DataWave combines CRM, CDP, CDPX, DMP and ESP functionality in a single platform, which lets you automate any marketing task. The platform is built on a microservice architecture with a ready-made API and extensive integration options. As a result, you can automate any marketing task.
