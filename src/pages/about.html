About the platform

A customer-centric platform for data-driven marketing

Data Wave combines advanced data analytics with multichannel campaign execution. The single customer profile is used for segmentation, analytics, launching personalized marketing campaigns and evaluating their effectiveness.

CRM, CDP, CDPX, DMP and ESP functionality work together in one platform, which lets you automate any marketing task. Our goal is to help you maximize profit and optimize marketing spend by reaching the right customers with the right content at the right time.

Data Wave serves as a single control center, a point for collecting statistics and a quality control system for customer service.
