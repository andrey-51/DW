Platform design update

The Datawave team has completed work on the new platform design, improving the user interface and making the platform easier to use.

The update aims to improve the platform's visual design and optimize how users interact with it.

What has changed?

  • Color palette and accents;
  • Animations and transitions;
  • Typography: new fonts for better readability;
  • Icons and graphics: updated icons, new shapes;
  • We have also completely reworked the UX of every module, changing the page structure and how content is organized.
