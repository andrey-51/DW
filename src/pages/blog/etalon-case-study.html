Etalon: CJM and marketing automation architecture

March 10, 2025 Real estate

A detailed customer journey map (CJM) is the foundation of effective marketing campaigns. For Etalon, it became the basis of a marketing automation architecture designed to raise conversion from inquiry to lead and actively move customers down the sales funnel.

Start with the customer's steps

A CJM has to show which steps the customer takes: where they came from and what they will do next. The Etalon map covers every stage of interaction, from searching for a home on property aggregator sites to follow-up communication after a meeting with a sales manager.

From the map to the automation architecture

Mapping the journey in this much detail makes it possible to work out the marketing automation architecture step by step. The goal is to attract marketing leads and warm them up until they become hot leads.

The channels behind the automation

The automation is built on four tools:

  • Email
  • Website page personalization
  • Personalized pop-ups (banners)
  • SMS

Together they make it possible to work with a customer at any stage of the funnel, from first interest to repeat communication.
